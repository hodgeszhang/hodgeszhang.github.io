## About Me

I am a Ph.D. researcher in Computer Science and Technology at **Nanjing University**.

My research interests focus on:

- **Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs)**
- **Unified Understanding-Generation Models**
- Vision-Language Models
- Multimodal Reasoning and Alignment

I have been selected for:

- Shanghai Qiming Star Talent Program
- Shanghai Oriental Talent Program

My research goal is to build next-generation multimodal foundation models with strong understanding, reasoning, and generation capabilities.
